What Terrasat does

Prediction, action, and prevention in one workflow

Terrasat helps Mathare anticipate flood risk early, coordinate response quickly, and support preventive work that reduces damage to homes, roads, and drainage systems.

  • PredictionUses ward-level flood indicators and geospatial data to flag areas that may become vulnerable.
  • ActionTurns those insights into maintenance tasks and operational follow-up so teams can respond faster.
  • PreventionSupports drainage clearing, infrastructure protection, and long-term resilience before severity increases.
